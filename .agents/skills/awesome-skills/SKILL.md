# Antigravity Awesome Skills Directory & Registry

A curated catalog and discovery engine for Antigravity, Claude Code, and Agentic AI skills.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Skill Discovery & Categorization
When addressing complex user tasks outside standard patterns, locate or scaffold skills across these primary categories:
- **Engineering & Architecture**: `graphify`, `claude-code`, `ralph-loop`, `coderabbit`, `playwright`, `debug`.
- **Design & UX Systems**: `taste`, `impeccable`, `twentyfirst-dev`, `componentry`, `ui-ux-pro-max`, `huashu-design`, `stitch`.
- **Productivity & Optimization**: `ponytail` (minimalism), `getshitdone` (velocity), `claude-seo` (search & GEO).
- **Orchestration & Autonomous Agents**: `manus` (cloud sync), `roo-code` (multi-mode), `skill-x` (dynamic loader).

### 2. Standardization Standard (`SKILL.md`)
Ensure all newly added skills conform to the canonical standard:
- **YAML Frontmatter**: Requires `name` (lowercase-hyphenated) and `description` (third-person trigger instructions).
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Detailed manuals and scripts partitioned into `references/` and `scripts/`.
- **Mirroring**: Deployed both locally in `.agents/skills/<name>/` and globally in `~/.gemini/config/skills/<name>/`.
