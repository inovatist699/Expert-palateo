Delete your Palateo account

Open Palateo, sign in, and choose Palate → Privacy and account → Delete my account. Confirm your password (or reverify with Truecaller if your account has no password) and permanent deletion.

This removes your account, taste profile, saved places, ratings, connections and associated account events. It also removes the waitlist entry for your account email and the conversation memory stored on the device used for deletion. Copies on other devices can be removed by clearing app storage.

Deletion cannot be undone. Provider backups may retain copies until their normal backup cycles expire. Any legally required retention is explained in our privacy policy.

Cannot access the app? Request deletion by email from your registered email address. Include “Palateo account deletion” in the subject. We verify ownership and aim to complete requests within 30 days. Never send your password.
