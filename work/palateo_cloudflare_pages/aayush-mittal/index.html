Building Palateo

Palateo helps people discover cafés, restaurants and resto-cafés in Ahmedabad and Vadodara with suggestions shaped around their food preferences, budget and dietary choices.

Aayush is building Palateo to make it easier to find local places that fit what someone wants to eat.
